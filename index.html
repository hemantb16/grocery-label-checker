    About

    What you see

    Watch out
    High sugar, fat, saturated fat or salt (UK Food Standards Agency levels), ultra-processed food, and colours that EU law says may affect children's attention.
    Good
    What the product would be allowed to claim on its pack under EU rules: no or low sugar, fat or salt, fibre, protein, low calories.
    Worth knowing
    Calories, sweeteners, caffeine, alcohol, and phenylalanine (for people with PKU).

    Values missing from the database are never counted as good. For allergies, always read the allergens printed in bold on the pack.

    Always read the pack

    Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a free database filled in by volunteers. It can be wrong, old, or about a different version of the product. Recipes change. This app is a second look, not a guarantee, and not medical advice.

    Privacy

    Recent scans are stored only in this browser. When you check a product, its barcode is sent to Open Food Facts, which (like any website) sees your IP address. Nothing else is sent, there is no tracking and no account.
